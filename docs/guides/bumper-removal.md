# Bumper Removal

Front bumper removal for the Alfa Romeo 159, Brera and Spider.

For Project Halo this matters because **the lower headlight mount can only be
reached with the bumper off the vehicle.**

What follows is the procedure as described by Alfa Romeo in eLearn, their
in-house dealer-level procedure software — the same descriptive and pictorial
guide used by Alfa Romeo trained technicians. Taken from the 159 procedures
catalogue, but it should apply across the range: 159 saloon and Sportwagon,
Brera and Spider.

*Comments added by JabawokJayUK are in italics.*

## Tools required

No special tooling — commonly available spanners and sockets only.

All bolts are listed in the procedure as **M6 × 22**, though depending on the
life of your vehicle, and more probably on what Alfa Romeo had in the factory at
the time, yours may differ.

*For example, my under-engine protection guard is held on with Torx bolts, not
M6.*

## The procedure

### Step 1

Position the vehicle on a lift *(or place it on axle stands)* and raise it.

*You can either turn the wheels on the steering rack or remove the wheels
entirely to reach the wheel arch liner bolts.*

Working on both sides of the vehicle, undo the bolts fixed to the wheel arches
**(1a)** and the nuts fixed to the bodyshell **(1b)**.

### Step 2

Undo the lower bolts fixing the bumper.

*These are the front three bolts linking the engine tray to the bumper. Be
careful — the mounts are very thin metal plates welded off the front of the
subframe and they rot into nothing at an alarming rate. If you've got the time,
give these a quick rust-proof while you're in there.*

### Step 3

Lower the vehicle *(only if you've got it up on a lift)* and undo the upper bolts
fixing the bumper.

### Step 4

Move the bumper **(1a)** slightly to one side by releasing it from the side
retainers.

*Essentially, with a good wiggle it should just pop off — but "move slightly to
one side" means don't run off with it just yet. It's still plugged in.*

Then disconnect the electrical connections.

*What isn't mentioned here, for some reason, is the headlight washers. You may
well have these nested in your front bumper and you'll need to disconnect the
pipe. If you do, crimp or bung the pipe or chances are you'll empty your washer
bottle all over the floor.*

## Conclusion

And that's a wrap, as they say in show business.

![Headlight location in the engine bay](../images/build/headlight-location-engine-bay.jpg)

While you're in there, clean dirt and grit out of any panel joins and hard to
reach places — particularly the join between the bumper and the wing — to extend
panel life and keep the dreaded tin worm out.
