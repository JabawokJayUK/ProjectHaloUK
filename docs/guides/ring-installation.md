# Installation of the Rings

Fitting the halo rings into the lens assemblies. This is the easy bit — each
ring is a near-exact size match for its lens, so they drop into the right place.

## Ring sizes

| Lens | Ring |
|---|---|
| Inner | 95 mm |
| Middle | 90 mm |
| Outer | 85 mm |

## Step 1 — Remove the in-line regulators

Each ring comes with a small black box attached to it. This is an in-line power
regulator — if you wired the ring straight to the car's battery, it is what
would stop the ring bursting into flames.

**Cut them off.** All of them.

The controller modules regulate the power instead, using Pulse Width Modulation
to vary intensity. If you leave the regulators in place the controller cannot
dim the rings and the whole thing fails fairly badly.

You want to be left with a ring and two wires, three times per headlight.

## Step 2 — Open the lens assembly

Cover the outer lens plastic with tape or similar to prevent accidental
scratches. Wear latex gloves whenever you handle the reflector or lens housing —
fingerprints and grease marks on the inside are permanent once it's sealed back
up.

Separate the reflector from the plastic lens by removing the **4 screws** holding
it in and gently pulling it apart.

![Prep](../images/build/rings-01-prep.jpg)
![Remove screws](../images/build/rings-02-remove-screws.jpg)
![Separate](../images/build/rings-03-separate.jpg)

## Step 3 — Place the rings

Put the correct ring into each lens and secure it with a small amount of glue on
the back edge of the ring. They're a tight fit, so glue is optional.

![Place 1](../images/build/rings-04-place.jpg)
![Place 2](../images/build/rings-05-place.jpg)
![Place 3](../images/build/rings-06-place.jpg)

## Step 4 — Route the ring wires

**Route the wires over the top of the headlight, not underneath.**

The bonnet slightly overhangs the light units on the car, so wires routed over
the top are invisible unless the bonnet is lifted. Routed underneath, you can
see a small amount of wire when looking at the lights on the car.

Make sure the wires from the rings sit towards the top of the headlight before
you close anything up.

![Cables 1](../images/build/rings-07-cables.jpg)
![Cables 2](../images/build/rings-08-cables.jpg)
![Cables 3](../images/build/rings-09-cables.jpg)

## Completion

Double-check every surface for fingerprints and smudges and clean as necessary —
this is your last chance. Then install the reflector housing back into the lens
unit and screw it together.

## A note on ring colour

When you order the rings, specify the colour carefully.

- **"White"** rings are about **5500 K** — a near-perfect match for HIDs.
- **"Cold white"** rings have an electric blue hint, closer to **7000 K**.
